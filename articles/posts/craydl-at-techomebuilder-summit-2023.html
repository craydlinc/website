Craydl Joins Industry Innovators at TecHome Builder Summit 2023

Craydl will attend the TecHome Builder Summit for the first time. This year the summit will be held in Phoenix, Arizona from November 6-8, 2023.

TecHome Builder Summit: Innovative Technology + Building Industry

We are excited to share our revolutionary platform and services, which offer preconstruction solutions to some of the industry's most pressing challenges while elevating communication and profit margins.

The TecHome Builder Summit is known for showcasing remarkable technological advancements for some of the most influential single-family homebuilders and multifamily companies. As a hub of innovation, the summit will bring together over 300 thought leaders, from multifamily developers, production builders, custom home builders, and luxury builders, who recognize the need to stay on the cutting edge of technological evolution.

The summit promises to be a melting pot of ideas, collaborations, and insights as industry leaders explore their technological options. This year Craydl and the TecHome Builder Summit join forces to push the boundaries of possibility in the world of homebuilding.

We hope you can attend the Summit. Please take a look at our Builder Innovator profile in the meantime.
